# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want Trace to be a welcoming, practical project. Participants must be
respectful, assume good faith, give actionable feedback, and make room for
different experience levels and backgrounds.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, doxxing, sexualized attention without consent, and publishing someone
else's private information or credentials.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation for behavior that violates this policy.
They will prioritize the safety and privacy of reporters.

Report concerns privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting or a
private message to the repository maintainers. Do not include secrets or other
sensitive material in a public issue.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
